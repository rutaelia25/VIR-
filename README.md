# VIR – Holographic Telepresence Robot

> A student-designed robotics project exploring remote presence, communication and interactive human-robot experiences.

## Overview

VIR is a conceptual intelligent telepresence robot designed to bridge the gap between physical and digital presence. It aims to enable remote users to interact with and experience distant environments through a combination of robotics, artificial intelligence, wireless communication, and human-computer interaction.

Its proposed capabilities include remote navigation, audio-visual communication, environmental awareness, and an interactive Presence Crown that serves as a defining visual element of the robot.

The physical design is being developed in Onshape, with a modular architecture consisting of a main body, mobility platform, Presence Crown, and dedicated spaces for electronic components.

VIR brings together mechanical engineering, embedded systems, AI, and robotics to explore a new approach to remote human presence.


## The Problem

VIR is designed to solve the "I am late for a very important meeting" as its sole and only reason for existence of VIR.

## Our Solution

VIR is pushed to create a visual hologram of the person to give others the sense of presence of the absent person in the board room.

- Remote communication
- holographic interactions
